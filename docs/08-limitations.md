inspect enough code, form the right mechanism, edit correctly or survive arbitrary process and billing failures.

Rapid rows reuse public tasks and stay `official=false`; one AnyIO task cannot establish general quality or transfer.
Unequal completion censors cost comparisons, and visible checks cannot identify hidden failures.

anchored matches may be irrelevant, and lifecycle fields prove neither understanding nor atomicity. Runner-continuity

orchestration, not Docker availability or provider compatibility. Its live inspect passed once, then strict-schema

`--pull never` failures cannot trigger reinspection or retry. Details remain in the historical audit and evidence index.

and provider acceptance remain unobserved. Dynamic mock correction/review is public smoke evidence, not AnyIO

R24 did observe provider-compatible initial requests, but it did not complete: only 3/6 rows settled and none reached
