## Question and decision

The implemented experiment compares a mandatory verification phase in the repair
conversation (A) with a fresh verifier conversation (B). Its earlier smoke test
reset A at entry too, leaving open whether a meaningful contrast survived. Budget
reservations could also remove the candidate or repair opportunity. Paying for
an experiment before checking those mechanisms would leave its result ambiguous.

Qualification finds a real entry contrast and minimum-path budget feasibility, but
also a separate return-delivery defect. B's selected public observations do not
reach repair in full. This blocks freezing the executable packet. The result does
not establish that an independent verifier helps or harms Astroid repair.

## Funding and candidate opportunity

All three captured pairs mechanically fund the minimum registered path: one
observation followed by a report, then the existing three-call mutation/check/
finish forecast. This calculation uses minimum output admission, not useful
reasoning. At 60k uncached input, a 128-output request costs USD 0.045576: each
USD 0.20 phase funds four such calls. Three calls cost USD 0.136728. Six calls with
25k output would cost USD 0.945 and do not fit. A first worst-input 25k-output call
can consume USD 0.1575 and leave too little even for the next minimum request.
Per-call admission remains unchanged; there is no new successor reservation.

Saved trajectories expose additional risk:

- A1: the next request after 26 calls / USD 0.44443305 cannot fit the initial
  USD 0.48 cap at its saved 48,133 input tokens. No edit exists at that point.
- B1: after 26 calls / USD 0.43272195, the next saved input admits only 655 output
  tokens versus its actual historical 1,196. Its ready candidate at sequence 401
  is therefore a useful same-state diagnostic, not a guaranteed reachable new run.
- B2: after 23 calls / USD 0.41640060, the next call admits 9,185 output tokens
  versus historical 10,895. The nominal cost-switch snapshot at sequence 350 has
  no edit and follows this earlier divergence.

Do not concatenate later saved decisions after an incompatible output ceiling and
call that a simulated successful trajectory. The new instructions/cache behavior
may change decisions and costs. The replay establishes risks and mechanical lower
bounds, not that two future rows must lack candidates or that repair will fail.

## Blocking return-path defect

A small task-independent local fixture runs the common repair, two public probes,
a registered read that selects those probes in the existing primary/countercheck
slots, a report, and final repair. Both mock runs reach isolated smoke evaluation.
The actual first final-repair inputs show:

| Return input | Selected probe bodies delivered | Question evidence status | Request bytes |
| --- | ---: | --- | ---: |
| A | 2/2 | complete / complete | 107,690 |
| B | 0/2 | incomplete / incomplete | 88,559 |

The bounded report itself arrives. Summary observations can remain in recent-probe
fields, but the selected complete program/result exchanges are missing from B.
This is not a measured size overflow or an additional reference request.

`JournalView.events()` filters the verifier stream out when returning to repair.
The ordinary `optional_exchanges()` lookup uses that filtered view, so it cannot
find selected verifier actions. In addition, `finish_verification` puts only
`focus_source_references()` in the report: selected tool-result references are not
carried there. Thus exclusion of verifier native history also excludes the public
evidence channel needed to audit its report. The existing generic report-delivery
test checked the report object, not these selected bodies.

The next change should carry only the selected, provenance-bound public exchanges
across this boundary, keeping both reference slots and existing overflow handling.
Do not copy verifier native continuation or plans, add evidence slots, extend the
budget, or assume the report establishes success. Requalify return delivery and
displacement after that fix, then perform final-implementation validation and freeze.
