## Why the final assessment stopped

At `caller_uptake_execution.py:277`, the frozen assessor constructs an
`ArtifactStore` rooted at `packet/artifacts` but asks it to write the source-only
patch under `packet/assessments/A-r1-t13`. That target is outside its owning root.
`write_bytes_atomic` correctly raises `RecoveryError: derived artifact path
escapes the artifact root`. No source-only patch was written, no caller probe or
native evaluation began, and no assessment retry occurred.

The panel journal records audit seal at sequence 65, assessment start at 66 and
assessment stop at 67. A separate provider-free reproduction confirmed both
the rejection and successful writing when the diagnostic target belongs to its
store. This diagnoses an operator path-ownership defect; it does not weaken the
path boundary or change the frozen assessor. The pre-run test suite did not catch
this real artifact-store path through submitted-patch assessment.

The original [stop rule](../../.agent/plans/caller-uptake-comparison.md) says an
assessment error stops remaining assessments and permits no assessment retry.
The original journal, sealed grades, annotations, manifest and failed assessment
are preserved. There is no fabricated `packet/result.json`; a separately labeled
stopped-study closeout records the available and missing outcomes.

## Decision and next bounded work

The available evidence supports **access without qualifying autonomous uptake**,
one observed caller-check scope error, and no registered repair-success gain.
It does not support increasing retained context, adding a third reference,
treating all failures as forgotten evidence, or estimating the effect of correct
caller use. Six repetitions of one development-exposed task are descriptive;
the missing post-run assessments further limit the result.

Keep the baseline and close this paid allocation. The next concrete engineering
work is the assessor's artifact-root defect, with a regression that crosses the
real store boundary. A separate provider-free follow-up could then assess the ten
saved submissions with the same frozen programs, environments and row limits,
preserving this stopped record. That follow-up and the source fix are **not run
or implemented by this closeout**. No additional paid solve is needed to recover
the missing assessment evidence; no new prompt/context experiment is proposed.
