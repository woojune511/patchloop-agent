recipe appears in the system text but explicitly applies only to a current
`working_plan.review_request.behavior_change`, not historical receipt copies.
Thus the structured reference-review contract was usable and used, but the
conditional post-mutation family recipe was **not active at this checkpoint**.
This must not be described as a test of that active post-mutation flow.

The old plan also says to check then submit. The last diagnostic message asks for
an advisory report of evidence and limitations within the remaining calls/actions,
and the action basis explicitly refers to that completion request. Those are
plausible influences favoring summary over exploration; one response cannot establish
their causal contribution or distinguish it from general action-selection behavior.

The next decision is therefore to align the diagnostic purpose with an actually
active review checkpoint before adding runtime rules or attributing the result to
reasoning ability. Another generic coverage reminder is not justified by this result.
A different checkpoint or fresh normal run would be a new question and scope;
neither was executed or authorized by the unused allocation.

The existing mechanism remains available as evidence-linked review, with no claim
that it fixes autonomous exploration. No new mandatory probe, finish gate, reviewer,
state store or runtime rule was added after this result. Counterexample construction
