## Delivery measurement limitation and source displacement

The frozen delivery detector has a confirmed false negative. It compares a raw
probe receipt's full `output` with the model projection. That projection
intentionally removes the ambiguous execution `status=passed` and adds a scoped
observation. A2's actual probe body at request 418 was consequently labeled
`OMITTED`. Ten subsequent requests retained exact stdout/stderr, question,
exit code and observation in `recent_probes`, but the detector did not recognize
that unbound-helper representation either.

A separate public-only supplement decodes JSON strings and compares the actual
fields: the body at 418 and observation summaries at 436, 455, 470, 487, 502, 517,
539, 556, 571 and 588 all preserve the complete helper stdout/stderr. Mutated
stdout and unrelated identity controls are rejected. Summary delivery is not
full setup/flag retention; after edits it remains a historical observation, not
current behavior. An earlier literal `contains()` check of the multiline stdout
also missed JSON newline escaping; it was not evidence of truncation.

Frozen annotations/results remain unchanged. Their automatic delivery and
supported-limitation-use tallies must not be quoted as proof of absence: the
separate review credits A2's limited helper interpretation in report 439.
This correction creates no original-caller verification, ON uptake, comparable
original-call pair or accepted repair. J and R conclusions remain unchanged.

Actual input inventories also retain source losses by path/version/line range:

- B1's last size handoff lost 125 versioned lines, 16 with equivalent text still
  present, including builder and module-resolution branches.
- B2's verification handoff lost 315 versioned lines, nine with equivalent text

These are within-run observations, not matched-checkpoint A/B treatment effects

final helper-to-caller inference error.

## Decision, validation and evidence

The prespecified mechanism signal requires faithful B use; observed use is zero.
Neither a favorable local signal nor general improvement is established. The
partial allocation and divergent trajectories do not justify estimating the
causal effect of successful linkage use. There is no new paid run queued.

Before another comparison, isolate the operator checkout at launch and make
offline delivery measurement understand the actual projection while preserving
identity, scope, currency and truncation checks. A further behavioral experiment
needs a reason tied to selecting the original caller, since extending linkage
after selection did not address that earlier failure here. These are next
questions, not changes implemented or additional experiments approved by this
record. The current transition/reference/resource rules remain unchanged.

239 quick tests including mock smoke, and **5,569 PASS / 20 SKIP / 0 FAIL** in the
full suite. This turn changed no product runtime or frozen grader code. Local
postprocessing provenance controls, projection controls, accounting reconciliation
and the nine saved-patch assessments completed; documentation validation is
recorded separately in the closeout journal.
