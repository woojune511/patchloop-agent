## Question and reason for this comparison

The [B diagnosis](../../docs/history/2026-10-19-caller-diagnosis.md) found that the
installed caller and its disclosure reached the model, but the original caller
arguments/failure did not. Some helper successes were used to justify broader
completion claims. That distinguishes a demonstrated scope error from an untested
claim that the model would ignore an adequately delivered original-caller trace.

At the same pre-decision state, does supplying accurate public caller observations
change the next judgment and improve the final repair? Distinguish autonomous
acquisition, actual delivery/retention, evidence-supported decisions, and repair.
Acquisition cost, attention, interpretation, implementation and remaining resources
are competing explanations. Supplying a trace does not by itself settle them.

The operator selected this exposed failure after inspecting old outcomes. This
study estimates the local effect of a supplied program/failure/control bundle on
continuations of one checkpoint. It is neither a fresh-solve success estimate nor
a general test of model reasoning capacity. The bundle includes saved acquisition
work; its benefit cannot be attributed solely to one trace field or reasoning skill.
