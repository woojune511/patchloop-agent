## Public audit before acceptance grades

Reuse the frozen public completion rubric in
[verification-state comparison](verification-state-comparison.md), including
CONTRADICTED, UNSUPPORTED, SUPPORTED_DIRECT, SUPPORTED_INFERENCE, UNKNOWN and
NO_CLAIM. Lack of a declared probe is not automatically an unsupported claim.
Code-derived SUPPORTED and required=UNKNOWN are not ground truth. Source inference
can be justified; a misleading claim must be established from actual public
evidence, with action/input/diff/receipt identities and literal excerpts.

Mask row codes and seal all six public annotations before opening fresh acceptance
grades. The schema can reveal an arm, so this is not fully blind. No encrypted
reasoning, private evaluation, reference patch, previous solution or this rubric
enters the solver context. Do not infer private thoughts from nonuse.

Review every unique probe attempt, including rejected attempts. Keep these stages
separate, with denominators for assigned rows, probe attempts and relevant opportunities:

1. Original-verification intent, independently reviewed from the public question,
   action and plan. Helpers and uncertain experiments remain separate.
2. Offered schema, chosen kind/declaration, admission, bound case and failure reason.
   Count model selection independently from whether the binding was executed.
3. Actual public caller/input/environment and expectation mapping: ORIGINAL,
   HELPER, UNKNOWN or UNOBSERVED. Self-labeling alone does not establish fidelity.
4. Comparable recorded result, missing/invalid observation, execution failure,
   setup change and exact before/after replay. A progress message is not a verdict.
5. Actual subsequent delivery and use: require a preceding bound receipt, its
   case/value in the dispatched input, a subsequent bound action/assessment and
   explicit semantic rationale. Field presence, naming a tool and declaration
   count alone do not earn use credit. Record useful historical scope reasoning
   separately from applying a current-diff comparison.
6. Completion judgment and independent repair acceptance. R requires the exact
   submitted patch to pass native and both missing/present caller controls.

The additional grader checks provenance and completeness, not semantic truth.
Its probe annotation retains independent intent/execution scope and rationale.
The common separation-exposure axes describe state available in both arms;
changed_mechanism_applied additionally requires observed comparison application.
Report the full stage counts even if the mechanism remains unexercised.

Report J/3 and R/3 with UNKNOWN, NOT_RUN, claims, submissions, costs, token/cache/
reasoning usage and timing. An aborted run is neither an observed repair failure
nor a justified abstention. Report newly absent caller/setup/countercondition and
probe-body evidence at transitions; divergent trajectories are descriptive, not
paired eviction experiments.

Keep the previous conservative screening margins: all six outcomes and required
assessments known, J_B <= J_A - 2, R_B >= R_A + 2, and supported current comparison
use in at least two accepted B repairs. Ambiguous B claims count against the
judgment margin; ambiguous A claims cannot create it. More bindings or better
judgment without accepted repairs supports only that mechanism stage. Three per
arm is exploratory and cannot establish statistical or general benefit. No
mid-run adjustment, extra condition or follow-up is authorized by inconclusiveness.
